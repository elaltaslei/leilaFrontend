THE EXPERIENCE

Mallorca & the islands

Let the coast unfold.

From Palma, the Balearic Islands open up a world of coves, harbours and changing horizons. Mallorca, Cabrera and other destinations can be considered according to the charter length, weather, draft and sailing conditions.

Every route is a conversation, and the final plan is confirmed with the conditions in mind.

At the table

Good food, good company.

For services where it is included, breakfast and one meal are served on board. Menus can be adapted to preferences and intolerances when shared in advance.

Drinks, provisioning, APA and any other extras depend on the agreed charter. What is included and what is charged separately will be set out with your confirmed offer.
